# Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to IndentView.

This document explains what you grant when you contribute, and why it is asked
for. It is short on purpose, and it is written to be read rather than skipped.

## Why this exists

IndentView is released under the AGPL-3.0. Academic and non-commercial use is
free and always will be.

Instrument manufacturers and other companies sometimes want to include this
software in a product that they cannot open-source. The AGPL does not permit
that, so they need a separate commercial licence — and the ability to grant one
is what funds continued work on the project.

Granting a commercial licence requires holding the rights to **all** of the
code. If contributors retain exclusive copyright over their patches, no such
licence can be granted without tracking down every past contributor for
permission. In practice that means it can never be granted at all.

This agreement solves that, and nothing else.

## What this does not mean

- It does **not** let anyone close the source of the public project. IndentView
  stays under the AGPL-3.0. Nothing in this agreement permits removing that.
- It does **not** transfer ownership of your work away from you.
- It does **not** restrict what you may do with your own code elsewhere.

## How to sign

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to its terms for this and future contributions.
```

That is all. One line, once.

If you would prefer not to agree, please say so in the pull request — a
contribution can still be discussed, and a maintainer may reimplement the idea
independently. Ideas are not covered by copyright; only their expression is.

that is a defect in the document and worth reporting.
